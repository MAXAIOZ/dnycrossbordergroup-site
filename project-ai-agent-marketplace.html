Project Profile

Australia AI Agent Marketplace

An industry-specialised marketplace for AI agents — built for Australian healthcare, legal, finance, mining, agriculture and education, where enterprises, SMEs and government can find AI agents suited to local rules and workflows.

SectorAI Marketplace
StageConcept & Market Analysis

Overview

Global platforms like Salesforce AgentExchange and Google Cloud's Agent Marketplace have shown what a large AI agent marketplace looks like — but they're built for global scale, not for Australian-specific rules and workflows.

This platform takes the opposite approach: industry specialisation and local compliance first. Agents are built and vetted for the rules that actually apply here — healthcare privacy requirements, financial-services compliance, mining safety data and agricultural workflows — rather than one generic agent trying to fit every market at once.

What sets it apart

  • Industry-specific compliance built in, not bolted on afterward
  • Local regulatory alignment — privacy, financial services, safety
  • Faster iteration than generalist global platforms
  • Trust and security features: audit trails and safety guardrails
Who It Serves

Three customer segments, one marketplace

01

Enterprise & Institutions

Banks, insurers, hospital groups and mining majors needing custom, high-reliability agents with the compliance and support their scale demands.

02

Small & Medium Enterprises

Customer-service and sales-lead agents at subscription or pay-per-use pricing, without the IT team a custom build would need.

03

Government & Public Sector

Agents aligned to Australia's national AI strategy and data-sovereignty requirements, for digital-service and public-facing use cases.
