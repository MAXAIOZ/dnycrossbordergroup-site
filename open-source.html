Say What You Mean By “Open”

Not every “open” model is the same

Open-source terminology is itself a risk. The platform distinguishes clearly between three categories and maintains a model licence register before commercial deployment.

Category

Open-source AI

Aligned with the Open Source Initiative’s Open Source AI Definition — freedoms to use, study, modify and share, with access to the preferred form for modification.

Category

Open-weight models

Weights are available for use, but terms, data information or modification rights may be more restricted than open-source.

Category

Source-available

Code or weights can be viewed or used under specific conditions that may limit commercial use or redistribution.

DNY does not claim that all “open” AI is free of licensing or IP risk, or that open models are always cheaper or safer than proprietary ones. Each model is evaluated and licence-checked before production.
