Industries

Built for Australia’s physical economy

Australian AI adoption is growing fast but unevenly — and it is lowest in exactly the physical-economy sectors where trusted, deployable AI creates the most value. We start with real industry data, not software demos.

First Vertical

The built environment

Our first industry block is the Australian Open Built Environment — covering building, materials, housing, commercial construction, engineering, modular and 3D-printed construction, design, construction, supply chain and the building lifecycle.

The goal is not to publish companies’ commercial data. It is an “open data structure + tiered, authorised access” mechanism that enables cross-enterprise, cross-platform and cross-system data interoperability while protecting commercial secrecy, privacy and IP.

Data model

Open building data

Materials, products, manufacturers, suppliers and distributors; project types, scale and stages; cost, schedule, procurement, logistics and inventory; environment, carbon and lifecycle data — unified so different sources can be reliably called by AI, design, procurement and project systems.

Standards

Open standards & indicators

Material performance; structure, fire, waterproofing, thermal, energy and acoustic indicators; environment and carbon; modular and precast; 3D-print construction; and a mapping between Australian and international standards.

Product library

Open building product library

Machine-readable product records — parameters, materials, applicable scenarios, performance, certification and test reports, environment data, BIM / CAD / 3D models — with unified product IDs and API access.

MMC

Modular & MMC library

Standard modules and components, nodes and load parameters, assembly rules, factory-manufacturing and QC, transport and site-installation parameters — an Australian Modular Building Open Library.

3D print

3D construction library

Print materials and mix parameters, equipment and process parameters, structural design and strength data, interfaces and file formats — an Open 3D Construction Library and Australian compliance pathways.

Supply chain

Construction supply chain

Manufacturers, suppliers, importers and distributors; warehousing, logistics, customs and delivery; a traceable Product → Manufacturer → Logistics → Warehouse → Project → Installation chain.

Beyond The First Vertical

Repeatable across the physical economy

Once the built environment block is complete, the same method extends to logistics, manufacturing, agriculture, energy, healthcare, education and other priority industries.

Logistics & warehousing

Supply-chain intelligence, warehouse automation, robotics and predictive operations — the sector with the lowest reported AI use and the highest deployment upside.

Enterprise & professional services

Agents, workflow automation and private / hybrid deployment for organisations that need governance and data control alongside capability.

Long-term direction

Turn one Australian industry after another into machine-readable, AI-callable, enterprise-connectable trusted infrastructure — and build Australia’s open AI collaboration capability.
