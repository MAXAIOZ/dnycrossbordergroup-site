Project Profile

DNY AI Media Hub

AI-driven media operations for cross-border sellers — product video, social content, digital humans and multilingual production, run as its own dedicated platform rather than a generic marketing add-on.

SectorAI Media & Marketing
StageLive · Revenue
Target MarketGlobal Sellers
ModelService-based

Overview

DNY AI Media Hub runs the group's AI-driven media operations — turning a manufacturer's product range and factory capability into content overseas buyers actually engage with. It operates as its own platform (dnyaimedia.com) with dedicated service and industry pages, rather than as a generic marketing agency bolted onto the group.

It's a live, revenue-generating venture, and one of the group's clearest links between the AI layer and real client work.

Industries served

  • Hospitality
  • Manufacturing
  • Building materials
  • Baby & child products
  • AI short-drama and entertainment content
Services

Four service lines, one platform

01

AI Product Videos

Product and case-study videos generated from existing photography and product data, built for overseas buyer audiences.

02

Social Media Content

Content built for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube — the platforms that now shape cross-border discovery and trust.

03

AI Digital Humans

AI presenters for product explainers and brand storytelling, without the cost of recurring on-camera talent.

04

Multilingual Content

Product materials, brand stories and marketing content localised for the languages a seller's real buyers use.
