DNY Building Supply

A live building-materials and supply-chain venture connecting suppliers, manufacturers, builders and trade partners — with an actual showroom and warehouse on the ground in Sydney, not just a catalogue site.

SectorBuilding Supply Chain
StageLive · Scaling
Target MarketAustralia
ModelTangible Goods

Overview

DNY Building Supply runs a 10,000 sqm display showroom and a 10,000 sqm warehouse in Moorebank, NSW — supplying builders, developers, designers and homeowners across tiles and surfaces, bathroom products, kitchen products, flooring, hardware and tools, and doors and windows.

It's the group's first physical-economy venture, and the clearest link between the platform's AI and data layers and a real, trading, tangible-goods business. Building materials is a large, recurring market — this venture is trading today and scaling, not slideware.
