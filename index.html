About DNY Cross Border Group

A platform connecting people, industries and global markets from Australia

DNY Cross Border Group is based in Australia and focused on building practical cross-border connections between innovation projects, enterprises, supply chains, regional platforms, and international markets.

The group supports founders, companies, trade partners, service providers, and project teams seeking to establish, expand, collaborate, or commercialize across Australia and global markets.

DNY is not a single trade company, a single AI tool company, a recruitment agency, or a narrow consulting service. It is a platform-oriented group that brings together innovation landing, industry collaboration, cross-border trade, supply chain connection, AI-enabled business services, international talent exchange, and regional project development.

Inspired by Australia's land and spirit of connection, DNY works to build meaningful pathways between people, industries, projects, and global opportunities that contribute to better communities and sustainable development.

Project 01

AI International Trade Digital Services Platform for ASEAN & Oceania

DNY Cross Border Group is developing a regional AI international trade digital services project focused on ASEAN and Oceania. The project connects China's AI service capabilities, cross-border digital trade foundation, and supply chain resources with market demand from Australia, New Zealand, ASEAN, and broader Oceania.

The platform supports Chinese companies expanding into regional markets while helping overseas enterprises access China's AI content creation, digital marketing, AI customer service, trade service, and supply chain collaboration ecosystem.

  • AI content creation for international trade
  • AI product video generation and digital presentation
  • AI quotation and customer service support
  • AI trade data management and knowledge base
  • Overseas enterprise access to China's AI service ecosystem
  • Regional business service centre development
  • ASEAN and Oceania market service connection

Project 05 — Sub-project

DNY HR Hub

DNY HR Hub is a people-focused project under DNY Cross Border Group, supporting international talent connection, business delegation programs, founder exchange, professional visits, industry learning, project-based learning, and practical cross-border cooperation.

It builds a bridge for entrepreneurs, young professionals, industry practitioners, business delegations, and innovation teams seeking to understand international markets, connect with industry resources, and explore real opportunities across borders.

DNY HR Hub is not positioned as a migration agency, recruitment agency, education agency, or employment placement service. Job opportunities may include selected roles and project-based positions where available. DNY does not guarantee employment outcomes, visa outcomes, or sponsorship.
  • International business delegation programs
  • Entrepreneur and founder exchange programs
  • Industry visit and study tour programs in Australia
  • Young professional exchange programs
  • Project-based learning and collaboration
  • Professional networking and business matching
  • Selected job opportunities and project-based roles where available
