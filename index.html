What DNY Is

A deployment layer, not another foundation model

We don’t compete by training a new frontier model. We create value at the commercialisation layer — making capable open AI safe, governed and production-ready for Australian enterprise and industry.

01

Trusted by design

A Trusted Data Space and verifiable infrastructure record provenance, versions, authorisation and audit — so every model call can be traced to its source, standard and permission. Governance is part of the product, not an afterthought.

02

Open by design

Multi-model, multi-cloud, open-source and open-weight. No structural dependency on a single model provider, cloud or vendor — models are evaluated and licence-checked before they ever reach production.

03

From software to physical AI

Enterprise agents through to robotics, edge devices and warehouse automation — proven in real industrial environments, not just software demos. Our flagship, AIHL, is where the platform meets the physical economy.

Where We Start

Built for Australia’s physical economy

Australian AI adoption is growing fast but unevenly — and it is lowest in exactly the physical-economy sectors where trusted, deployable AI creates the most value.

Vertical 01

Built environment

An Australian Open Built Environment data model — open building product data, standards mapping, and modular / 3D-construction libraries that AI, design and procurement systems can call reliably.

Vertical 02

Logistics & warehousing

Supply-chain intelligence, warehouse automation and predictive operations — the sectors where reported AI use is lowest and the deployment upside is highest.

Vertical 03

Enterprise & services

Agents, workflow automation and private / hybrid deployment for organisations that need governance and data control alongside capability.
