About DNY Cross Border Group

A platform connecting people, industries and global markets from Australia

DNY Cross Border Group is based in Australia and focused on building practical cross-border connections between innovation projects, enterprises, supply chains, regional platforms, and international markets.

The group supports founders, companies, trade partners, service providers, and project teams seeking to establish, expand, collaborate, or commercialize across Australia and global markets.

DNY is not a single trade company, a single AI tool company, a recruitment agency, or a narrow consulting service. It is a platform-oriented group that brings together innovation landing, industry collaboration, cross-border trade, supply chain connection, AI-enabled business services, international talent exchange, and regional project development.

Inspired by Australia's land and spirit of connection, DNY works to build meaningful pathways between people, industries, projects, and global opportunities that contribute to better communities and sustainable development.

Compliance Boundaries

Clear lines on what DNY does — and does not — do

DNY coordinates execution and verification. Regulated advice and licensed services stay with qualified specialists.

Professional Services

DNY coordinates lawyers, accountants, tax/migration advisers, financial institutions and property/construction professionals. DNY does not itself provide legal, tax, migration, investment, financial-product, building or engineering advice without the relevant licence.

Payments & Finance

DNY can verify invoices and payees, match orders, generate payment-approval requests, coordinate licensed payment/banking institutions, and record outcomes. DNY does not custody client funds, run unlicensed FX exchange, or market unlicensed cross-border payment or financial services.

Verification Records

Verifiable records prove that a document was submitted or registered at a given time. Real-world truth comes from DNY's execution process, human checks and evidence standards — not from technology alone.

AI Agent Responsibility

Agents analyse and initiate tasks; clients set budgets, permissions and approvals; DNY executes and verifies; high-risk actions require human approval. Agents are not independent Australian legal entities and do not auto-sign contracts or make unlimited payments.
