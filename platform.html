Design Principles

How the platform is built

Multi-model by design

Avoid structural dependency on a single model provider; evaluate models and agents before production use.

Deploy anywhere

Multi-cloud and private / hybrid where commercially and technically feasible, with a clear separation of customer, operational and third-party data.

Enterprise-grade

Identity, permissioning, observability and logging as first-class features — not optional add-ons.

Licence-aware

A component inventory and model licence register underpin every deployment, because “open” is used inconsistently in the market.

API-first integration

Connect to legacy and modern enterprise systems through documented, standardised interfaces.

Human oversight

Escalation and review for use cases where full autonomy is inappropriate or unsafe.
