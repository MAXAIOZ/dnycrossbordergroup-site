Robotics & Physical AI

A commercialisation layer for robotics

A robotics and intelligent-device validation environment serves as a commercialisation layer for international and Australian products — supporting model integration, localisation, technical testing, enterprise demonstration, user feedback, channel validation and support-model design.

Flagship venture

Open-Source AI Robotics Experience & Innovation Centre

A physical showcase and Australian market-entry environment for advanced global robotics and AI hardware — humanoids, robot dogs and commercial service robots, an AI-home living lab, education and B2B demonstration. Products are selected on a global, multi-origin basis by technical maturity, Australian compliance, experience, after-sales and commercial sustainability.
