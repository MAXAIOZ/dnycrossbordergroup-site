What it is

The Australian Trusted AI Open Source Platform is the product form of DNY’s Trusted & Open AI Infrastructure: an open-core platform that is model-agnostic, security-isolated and capable of private deployment, built around skills, workflows and agent-as-a-service delivery.

The reusable core is intended for permissive open-source release. A separate commercial enterprise layer covers security, hosting, operations, compliance and advanced services.

Seven reusable capabilities

  • Industry data
  • Trust and rights
  • Standards and semantics
  • Trusted identity
  • API and integration
  • AI and agents
  • Transactions and workflows
Application Discovery

From verified news to audited application directions

A recurring discovery cycle monitors Australian and international sources and converts real events into concrete open-source application opportunities — each recorded with a full audit trail.

  5. Platform capability
  6. Six-dimension score
  7. Validation action
Source discipline

Every claim is attributed

Outlet, author, publication time and original link are recorded for every source event.

Information boundary

Fact separated from inference

Each record separates confirmed fact, analysis, inference and open questions. Organisations named in news are recorded as news subjects, not partners.

Scoring

Six dimensions

Pain intensity, public or commercial value, data availability, technical feasibility, platform reuse and ability to pay — used for ordering, not as market validation.

Governance

A proposed member-governed structure

DNY is evaluating a member-governed DAO structure for the open-source application programme, so that developers, validators, data contributors and domain sponsors can take part in decisions about which application directions move from concept to prototype to pilot — with those decisions kept on record.

Status

The platform is in development. The DAO structure is a proposal only: its legal wrapper, membership, governance and contribution terms are not settled. Application directions are concepts, not projects in delivery. Nothing on this page is an offer of securities, tokens or any financial product.
