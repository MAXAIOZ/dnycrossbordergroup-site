Overview

DNY AI Media Hub runs the group's AI-driven media operations — turning a manufacturer's product range and factory capability into content overseas buyers actually engage with. It operates as its own platform (dnyaimedia.com) with dedicated service and industry pages, rather than as a generic marketing agency bolted onto the group.

It's a live, revenue-generating venture, and one of the group's clearest links between the AI layer and real client work.
