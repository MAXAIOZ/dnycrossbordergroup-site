Overview

{{name}} is the group’s electric-vehicle accessories business. It designs and supplies fitted interior and cargo accessories for the EVs Australians are buying now — cut for the exact make, model and year, for Australian-delivered right-hand-drive vehicles.

It runs as two channels on one data set: a retail store for EV owners and a trade channel for dealers, leasing providers, fleets, detailers and OEM/ODM partners. Both share the same product, vehicle-fitment and order records, with separate pricing and customer journeys.

What sets it apart

  • New models first — fitment is planned for new EVs as they reach Australian showrooms, not years later
  • Built for Australian heat — heat-stable TPE rather than XPE foam, and clip-in roof shades rather than suction cups
  • Model-specific fit — every fitted product is listed against a named make, model and year, including mid-cycle updates
  • Planned Australian stock — prices in AUD including GST, with full Australian Consumer Law rights
  • Supply through the OPC + FDE network — manufacturing, OEM/ODM and small-batch custom production

Product Roadmap

Released in stages, compliance first

Class A — Non-electrical

Floor mats, liners, sunshades, organisers and seat protection. This is the launch range.

Class B — Electronic accessories

Portable chargers, Type 2 cables and V2L adapters first. Released only after supplier registration and RCM marking under Australia’s Electrical Equipment Safety System.

Class C — Lithium-battery products

Under assessment. Introduced only after battery testing, transport and insurance requirements are confirmed.
