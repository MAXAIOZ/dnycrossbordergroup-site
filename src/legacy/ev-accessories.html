Overview

{{name}} is the group’s electric-vehicle accessories business. It designs and supplies fitted interior and cargo accessories for the EVs Australians actually drive — cut for the exact make, model and year, for Australian-delivered right-hand-drive vehicles.

It runs as two channels on one data set: a retail store for EV owners and a trade channel for dealers, detailers, fleets and OEM/ODM partners. Both share the same product, vehicle-fitment and order records, with separate pricing and customer journeys.

What sets it apart

  • Model-specific fit — every fitted product is listed against a named make, model and year
  • Personalisation — base colour, stitching and embroidered initials, previewed before ordering
  • Australian consumer protections — prices in AUD including GST, with full Australian Consumer Law rights
  • Supply through the OPC + FDE network — manufacturing, OEM/ODM and small-batch custom production
