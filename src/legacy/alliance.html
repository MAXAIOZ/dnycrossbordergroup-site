Separation Principles

An ecosystem, without ownership confusion

Distinct entities

The Alliance and the commercial company (the investable business) are distinct legal and economic interests. Any related-party arrangement between them is documented on arm’s-length terms with appropriate conflict disclosure.

DNY does not imply exclusive access to the Alliance or its members, and Alliance partners do not endorse the commercial company without a written agreement.

Permitted collaboration

  • Joint research and industry programs under documented agreements
  • Developer events and technical education
  • Industry working groups and standards discussions
  • University collaboration and talent development
  • Global technology partner engagement
  • Demonstration projects and technical sandboxes
Purpose

Building Australia’s open AI capability

Research & developers

Support open AI research, developer engagement and technical education across Australia.

Responsible governance

Advance responsible, well-governed open AI adoption and standards discussion.

Global partnerships

Connect Australian capability with international open AI research, industry and technology partners.

Status

The Australian Open Source AI Alliance is proposed. Any participation, membership or partnership will be established through documented agreements. Individuals are described as “invited” or “proposed” until they have formally accepted.
