Overview

Small, high-volume, low-unit-value goods — home goods, small hardware, accessories, seasonal items — are exactly the category cross-border e-commerce sellers struggle to fulfil quickly and cheaply. Forward-deployed overseas warehouses are the standard fix, but most run on the same manual processes that created the problem in the first place.

This warehouse combines warehouse, order and transport management systems with AI vision quality checks and robotics-assisted picking, targeting a 99.5%+ on-time dispatch rate and 99%+ pick accuracy — moving goods from arrival to doorstep faster and with fewer errors.

Who it's for

  • Cross-border e-commerce sellers needing a forward-deployed overseas warehouse
  • Small-goods brands wanting faster local fulfilment
  • Trade partners needing verifiable inventory and dispatch records for financing
