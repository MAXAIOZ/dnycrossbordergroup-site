Positioning & Discipline

What we seek — and what we don’t claim

Defensible advantages sought

  • Australian enterprise and industrial relationships
  • Reusable deployment and governance stack across models
  • AILH and physical-industry proof points
  • Integrated software + agent + robotics capability
  • Local support and accountability
  • Accumulated implementation data and know-how

What we do not claim

  • That it has no competitors
  • That all “open” AI is free of licensing or IP risk
  • That open models are always cheaper or safer
  • That Alliance partners endorse the company without a written agreement
  • That AILH is owned or controlled by DNY unless formally documented
Structure & Governance

Before any raise

Corporate structure

The investable business is housed in a clearly identified Australian operating company that owns or validly licenses the core commercial platform IP.

IP chain of title

Software, datasets, prompts, agent templates, brands and documentation are audited, with written assignment or licence from founders, employees and contractors, and open-source components inventoried.

Data rights

Customer data is not a platform asset merely because it is processed; contracts define ownership, processing, training permissions and cross-border transfer.

Governance priorities

Founder / shareholder roles, conflicts and related-party policy, separation of platform from Alliance, financial reporting, and cyber, privacy, AI-governance and open-source licence policies.
