Overview

DNY Aerial Systems is the group’s commercial drone business. Aircraft enquiries are open. Specific models, supplier arrangements and delivery terms are confirmed after review. Operational drone services are not currently offered.

Aircraft and components are sourced on a multi-vendor basis for construction, agriculture and inspection applications.
