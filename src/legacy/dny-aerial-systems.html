Overview

DNY Aerial Systems is the group’s commercial drone venture and the first industry programme under the Open Physical Intelligence Layer. It is intended to serve government, infrastructure and enterprise customers by combining imported aircraft and components with Australian systems integration, contract delivery and after-sales support.

The venture can operate under its own brand, company and business model while reusing DNY’s trusted AI infrastructure — so that inspection data, AI analysis and mission records share one verifiable foundation.

Intended services

  • Aircraft and component supply on a multi-vendor basis
  • Australian systems integration for payloads, sensors and software
  • Inspection, mapping and data-capture programmes
  • Training, maintenance and lifecycle support
  • AI analysis and reporting through the Physical Intelligence Layer
