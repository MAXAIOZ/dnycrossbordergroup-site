Standards mapping layer

An Australian Standards Mapping layer structurally maps products, technologies and standards from different countries to Australian-applicable requirements, preserving source, version and update records.

Data rights & authorisation

Contracts define ownership, permitted processing, retention, model-training permissions, cross-border transfer, sub-processors and deletion — customer data is not treated as a platform asset simply because it is processed.
