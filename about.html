About DNY Cross Border Group

A venture platform that turns cross-border demand into backable projects

DNY Cross Border Group is an Australia-based platform group that builds, operates and scales a portfolio of cross-border ventures. Each venture is structured as a focused business — with a clear market, a real product, and execution on the ground.

Most cross-border businesses fail on execution, not ideas. DNY exists to close that gap. We identify high-demand cross-border needs — trade infrastructure, finance, supply chain, media and talent — and package each one into a venture with its own product, market and growth path.

Rather than a single trade company or a single AI tool, DNY is a platform-oriented group: a shared operating engine underneath, and a portfolio of backable ventures on top. That structure lets each venture move fast while drawing on common infrastructure, corridors and execution capability.

For founders, partners and prospective collaborators, DNY offers a single point of entry into a diversified set of cross-border ventures between Australia and global markets.
