Project 04
In Design

Australia AI Oracle

A trusted, rights-verified data platform giving AI agents and smart systems reliable access to Australian pricing and index data — commodity and trade price indices, tariff reference data and more, with every figure traceable back to its source.

Unlike a typical commercial data vendor, Australia AI Oracle is organised as a not-for-profit DAO — governed by an oracle council rather than run for private profit. Any fees collected go entirely toward operating the platform and supporting the development of Australia's trade infrastructure.

SectorData Infrastructure
StageIn Design
Target MarketAustralia
ModelNot-for-profit · DAO
  • Verified Australian commodity and trade price indices
  • Tariff, customs and export-compliance reference data
  • Cryptographic proof of source for every data point
  • Built for AI agents and smart systems to call directly
  • Governed by an independent oracle council, not a single operator
Not-for-profit DAO-governed Verifiable data
