Project Profile

Facadia — AI Materials Valuation

AI-powered residential facade intelligence — snap a photo of a home's exterior and get back a materials report and a budget estimate, instead of a manual quantity take-off.

SectorAI Product · PropTech
StageLive · Beta
Target MarketAustralia · Residential
ModelFreemium SaaS

Overview

Facadia turns a single photo into a materials report — what's on a home's facade, roughly what it would cost to replicate or renovate — using AI vision analysis rather than a manual estimate. It runs as a public web app, with a free tier for casual use and a paid plan for homeowners, renovators and building-materials suppliers who need more analyses.

Facadia also feeds the rest of the platform: its material identification and valuation output is a data source for the Oracle's building-materials verification work, and supports DNY Building Supply's product presentation.

Pricing

  • Free — 3 analyses per month, 1 image per analysis
  • Paid (A$19/month) — 20 analyses per month, up to 3 images per analysis
  • Extra credit packs available for occasional heavier use
  • Invalid images and failed analyses never consume credits
Core Features

Built as a real product, not a demo

01

AI Photo Analysis

Upload a photo and get a materials and finishes breakdown, powered by AI vision analysis in a single combined validation and analysis call.

02

Budget Estimation

An automatic budget estimate is generated alongside the materials report, giving a starting point for renovation or new-build planning.

03

Privacy by Design

EXIF and GPS data are stripped before storage and analysis. Reports and images are user-deletable, and analytics data carries no personal information.
