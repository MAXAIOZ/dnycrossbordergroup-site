Project Profile

AI Robotic Logistics Warehouse

A smart, robotics-driven fulfilment warehouse for cross-border small-goods e-commerce — built to hit industry-leading pick accuracy and turnaround speed.

SectorLogistics & Fulfilment
StageImplementation Planning
Target MarketCross-border E-commerce
ModelFulfilment-as-a-Service

Overview

Small, high-volume, low-unit-value goods — home goods, small hardware, accessories, seasonal items — are exactly the category cross-border e-commerce sellers struggle to fulfil quickly and cheaply. Forward-deployed overseas warehouses are the standard fix, but most run on the same manual processes that created the problem in the first place.

This warehouse combines warehouse, order and transport management systems with AI vision quality checks and robotics-assisted picking, targeting a 99.5%+ on-time dispatch rate and 99%+ pick accuracy — moving goods from arrival to doorstep faster and with fewer errors.

Who it's for

  • Cross-border e-commerce sellers needing a forward-deployed overseas warehouse
  • Small-goods brands wanting faster local fulfilment
  • Trade partners needing verifiable inventory and dispatch records for financing
Core Systems

Built as connected systems, not isolated tools

01

WMS + OMS + TMS

Integrated warehouse, order and transport management for multi-channel fulfilment — Amazon, eBay, Shopee, Temu and DNY's own channels.

02

AI Vision Quality Checks

Camera-based inspection at receiving and dispatch to catch damage, mislabelling and packaging issues before they become customer complaints.

03

Robotics-Assisted Picking

Goods-to-person and person-to-goods robotics (AMR/AGV) to speed up picking, sorting and consolidation during peak periods.
